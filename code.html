Frequently Asked Technical Questions

Everything you need to know about our automotive manufacturing certifications, mold tolerances, and prototyping turnaround.

We accept all standard 3D solid model formats including STEP (.stp, .step), Parasolid (.x_t), IGES (.igs), and native Siemens NX or CATIA files. For 2D drawing reviews and critical geometric tolerances (GD&T), vector PDF or DWG/DXF files are preferred. All uploaded files are stored in our encrypted ISO-compliant sandbox.
Rapid aluminum/soft-steel prototype tooling with T1 sample molded parts can be dispatched within 7 to 14 business days. For hardened Class 101 production multi-cavity hardened steel molds (1,000,000+ cycle warranty), standard delivery is 4 to 6 weeks, which includes comprehensive DFM, moldflow, and initial CMM validation.
Yes. Both facilities maintain independent IATF 16949:2016 and ISO 9001:2015 certifications. Our metrology rooms are equipped with optical coordinate measuring machines (CMM), melt flow indexers, moisture analyzers, and computerized universal tensile testers to execute full Level 3 PPAP audits.
